## SSRN (gives a DOI of the form 10.2139/ssrn.NNNNNNN)

1. Sign in at https://www.ssrn.com (create a free account if needed) and go to **My Papers** > **Submit a paper**.
2. Upload `inspection_rd.pdf`. Accept the copyright and distribution terms only after reading them; SSRN distributes the PDF publicly.
3. Enter the title, abstract, keywords and JEL codes above. Add yourself as author with the affiliation above.
4. Classification: choose Economics Research Network (ERN) and, if offered, eJournals covering urban economics, housing, regulation and public economics. SSRN's editors may adjust the classification.
5. Submit. SSRN reviews the paper (often a few business days) before it appears and before the DOI is active.
6. When it is approved, copy the abstract-page URL and the DOI.

## MPRA (Munich Personal RePEc Archive; gives a permanent MPRA paper number and URL)

1. Register at https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de and confirm the e-mail.
2. **New Item**, type **Paper**. Upload the PDF.
3. Enter title, abstract, keywords and JEL codes above; add yourself as author with your e-mail so RePEc can link the paper to you.
4. Deposit. MPRA editors check submissions before they go live.
5. To my knowledge MPRA assigns a permanent paper number and URL rather than a DOI. If the DOI matters for your records, cite the SSRN DOI and list the MPRA URL alongside it.
6. Optional: register with RePEc Author Service (https://authors.repec.org) and claim the MPRA paper so it appears on your RePEc profile.

## After submitting

- Send Claude the SSRN DOI and the MPRA URL; they go into the paper's first-page note, the README and `CITATION.cff`.
- Add a link to the paper on the OSF registration page (OSF lets you add a resource such as a preprint to a registration).
- Write the evidence-log rows the same day (date, venue, link or DOI, status, saved confirmation).
